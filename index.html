How Each State Sells Delinquent Property

Click a state to open its rules and county list. Four systems exist: the county sells the property itself (tax deed), sells a lien that earns interest until the owner redeems (tax lien), sells a deed the owner can still buy back for a set time (redeemable deed), or does both in sequence (hybrid).
